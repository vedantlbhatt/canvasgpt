Canvas session cookie

Georgia Tech disables student access tokens, so the sync rides your browser session instead. The cookie dies when you log out of Canvas or after a few weeks — when it does, paste a fresh one here. No redeploy needed.

Stored on the server's disk, never shown again, never given to the chat model.

How to copy the cookie

  1. Open gatech.instructure.com in Chrome and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Press Cmd+Option+I to open DevTools, then click the Network tab.
  3. Reload the page. Click the very first request in the list (it'll be named dashboard or similar).
  4. In the panel that opens, scroll to Request Headers and find the cookie: line.
  5. Right-click that line → Copy value. Paste the whole thing above.
It's a live credential for your student account. This app only ever sends GET requests with it — it can read your Canvas, never change it.
